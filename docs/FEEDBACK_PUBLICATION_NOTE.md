# Publishing assessment feedback safely

Marked result: 98 / 100 (OBOP102 FA1, 2026).

The original marked assessment PDF has **not** been included in this GitHub-ready package. Before publishing feedback, check for and remove student number, signatures, personal details, internal assessment information, or other identifiers you do not want indexed publicly. Ensure your institution permits publication of assessment and assessor material.

Recommended portfolio presentation: a short verified result excerpt or an anonymized, flattened PDF extract, not the whole assignment paper and marking guide. Keep the original PDF privately as supporting evidence for interviews.
